# Project Submission Summary

## Project Title
Supermarket Sales Analysis

## Objective
Analyze supermarket sales data to identify useful information about products,
branches, categories, customers, payments and ratings.

## Dataset
500 supermarket sales transactions from the supplied source PDF.

## Technologies
Python, Pandas, Streamlit and Plotly.

## Main Analysis
- Product-wise sales
- Branch/city-wise sales
- Category-wise sales
- Payment-method usage
- Customer-type spending
- Customer ratings
- Monthly sales trend
- Sales formula validation

## Source-based results
Cheese generated the highest product sales (₹27,906.30).
Branch C (Mumbai) had the highest branch sales (₹72,469.45).
Beverages had the highest category sales (₹56,108.24).
UPI was used for 127 transactions.
Average Member transaction was ₹483.14 and Normal customer average was ₹497.07.
Average rating was 3.99 out of 5.

## Conclusion
The project converts raw supermarket transactions into visual and
actionable business insights through data cleaning, aggregation and
interactive visualization.
